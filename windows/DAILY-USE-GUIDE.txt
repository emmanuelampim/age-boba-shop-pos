DAILY USE GUIDE - AGE BOBA SHOP POS
===================================

This guide is for the cashier and the owner. It explains a normal day
with the POS: opening, selling, Mobile Money, closing, and looking up
yesterday's sales. Nothing in this guide needs a computer expert.

--------------------------------------------------
1. OPENING THE SHOP
--------------------------------------------------
1.1  Turn the computer on and let the person log in.
1.2  Wait about 10 seconds. The cashier screen opens by itself.
1.3  If it does not open: double-click  C:\boba-pos\windows\start_pos.cmd
1.4  Sign in with your own user (name + password). The cashier
     normally only needs a Cashier account. Get it from the owner.
1.5  You are logged in when the grey bar at the top shows your name.

--------------------------------------------------
2. SELLING A DRINK (CASH)
--------------------------------------------------
2.1  Make sure you are on the "New Sale" screen.
2.2  Tap the drink category (e.g. Milk Tea), then the drink.
2.3  Choose a size (Small/Medium/Large) if the drink offers sizes.
2.4  Add toppings if the customer wants them.
2.5  Change the quantity with the + and - buttons (or set 1).
2.6  The basket on the right shows the running total. Check it with
     the customer before paying.
2.7  Choose the payment method. For cash; choose Cash.
2.8  Press "Charge" / "Complete sale". The sale is now saved; it can
     never be lost.
2.9  Confirm the change given, then the receipt prints. Hand it over.

--------------------------------------------------
3. SELLING A DRINK (MOBILE MONEY - IMPORTANT)
--------------------------------------------------
3.1  Confirm the payment has ACTUALLY arrived before finishing:
     ask the customer to show you the confirmation notification on
     their phone, or check the money notification on your own phone.
3.2  In the POS, choose Mobile Money as the payment method.
3.3  Type the customer's phone number OR the last 4 digits shown on
     their phone notification (e.g. 4821).
3.4  Tick the box "Payment received" ONLY when the money has really
     arrived.
3.5  Finish the sale. The phone number/reference is saved with the
     order (the full reference is hidden; only last 4 digits show).
3.6  If the money has NOT arrived yet, do NOT tick the box - wait for
     the customer to send it.
3.7  The POS will refuse to complete a Mobile Money sale that has no
     phone number/last-4 digits or no confirmation. That is on
     purpose: it protects the shop from fake payments.

--------------------------------------------------
4. WRONG ORDERS / CANCELLING
--------------------------------------------------
4.1  Before finishing: use the trash/remove on the basket line and
     ring it again.
4.2  After finishing, go to Sales, find the order.
     - Cancel: removes the sale and gives the stock back (money
       returned to the customer).
     - Refund: keeps the sale recorded but marks it refunded.
4.3  The POS ALWAYS asks for a reason ("customer changed their mind",
     etc.). Type it - this is the record of WHY, in case of disputes.
4.4  Only an owner or manager can cancel/refund. Ask them to do it.

--------------------------------------------------
5. DASHBOARD / QUICK LOOK
--------------------------------------------------
5.1  The dashboard shows, for TODAY:
     - money taken so far and number of sales
     - Cash vs Mobile Money split
     - best-selling drinks
     - low-stock warnings (e.g. "Cups: 12 left")
5.2  Anyone can look. Only the owner/manager changes things.

--------------------------------------------------
6. LOOKING UP YESTERDAY'S / ANY DAY'S SALES
--------------------------------------------------
6.1  Click Sales.
6.2  Pick a date range (or use the search box for an order number
     or a note).
6.3  Every sale shows: time, items, payment method, staff, status.
6.4  Click an order to see its full detail, including the last 4
     digits of a Mobile Money reference and any cancellation reason.
6.5  Download a spreadsheet of any date range:
     click "Download report (Excel)" - a file downloads that opens in
     Excel / LibreOffice / Google Sheets. One row per item sold.
     Send the file to anyone who needs it; no internet needed.

--------------------------------------------------
7. STOCK AND MENU SMALL CHANGES (owner/manager only)
--------------------------------------------------
7.1  Products: change a price, hide a drink (Inactive), add a new
     drink/size/topping. Do NOT delete products - keep them Inactive.
7.2  Inventory: record deliveries (Restock) and waste (Waste).
     The program warns when an item gets low.
7.3  Settings > Users: add/disable staff accounts; change passwords.
     Remember: you cannot remove the last active owner - that is on
     purpose.
7.4  Settings > Shop/Hours: edit the shop name, hours, receipt text.

--------------------------------------------------
8. CLOSING FOR THE DAY (do this every night)
--------------------------------------------------
8.1  When the shop closes, click "Close for the day" at the bottom-
     left of the menu bar.
8.2  Confirm you are closing.
8.3  Wait for the green "All sales saved" screen. It means:
       - every sale is saved
       - a full backup file was written automatically
         (backend\data\backups\pos-YYYY-MM-DD.db)
       - a spreadsheet of the day is saved
         (backend\data\backups\sales-YYYY-MM-DD.csv)
       - if a pen drive is plugged in, those files were ALSO copied
         onto the pen drive
8.4  It then turns the server off. Now (and only now) it is safe to
     switch off the computer.
8.5  If someone forgot and the shop needs to keep going: just turn the
     computer on again and open the POS - today's data is still all
     there. "Close for the day" only makes the daily backup copy.

--------------------------------------------------
9. LOCKED OUT / IDLE / LONG GAPS
--------------------------------------------------
9.1  If the screen shows "Session expired" or login again: just sign
     in again with your own name and password. All sales are saved;
     nothing is lost.
9.2  The computer can be left on overnight; the POS does not expire
     sales. If you want to log out at night, click your name → Logout.

--------------------------------------------------
10. NO INTERNET, POWER CUTS, ETC.
--------------------------------------------------
10.1  The POS works without internet, always (once it is installed).
10.2  Power cut mid-sale: every finished sale was already saved to
      disk. On restart, open the POS - everything is there.
10.3  Computer freezes: turn it off with the power button, turn back
      on, log in, the POS opens by itself. Nothing to recover - every
      confirmed sale is already saved.

--------------------------------------------------
THE ONE-LINE RULE
--------------------------------------------------
Finish each sale fully, tick Mobile Money only when the money has
really arrived, cancel/refund with a reason, and close for the day
every evening so the backup copy is made.
